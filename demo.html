Things to try

The demo league is already part-way through, so there is something to do straight away.

  1. 1

    Play a match as a player

    Open the player view, submit a score for your next match, then use "You are" at the top to switch to the other team and confirm it.

  2. 2

    Run it as the organiser

    Add a team, generate fixtures, enter a score yourself, and sort out the score the teams disagree on.

  3. 3

    Fast-forward the season

    Tap "Simulate next week" to play a whole week at once, watch the table move, then start the playoffs and crown a champion.

What the demo leaves out

It is a practice league, so it is capped at 8 teams, there are no entry fees or payments, and it only exists in the browser you opened it in. A real league has your own teams, private links for every team, entry fees paid by card, and runs for as long as you need.
