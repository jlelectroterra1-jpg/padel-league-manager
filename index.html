Questions

Do players need an account?

No. Each team has a private link. One partner can accept the rules and pay for both of them.

How do players pay?

On their team link, through PayFast, by card or instant EFT. Padel League Manager never sees or stores card details.

What if a team doesn't pay by the cut-off date?

Their link shows that payment has closed, and you decide what to do: extend the date or remove the team.

What about refunds?

Refunds for teams that withdraw are handled by the league organiser.

Can I run a free league?

Yes. Leave the entry fee empty. You still pay the once-off R50 to activate the league.

Where do the court bookings happen?

Court fees are paid separately, directly to the court, when you book on the Playtomic app. Padel League Manager doesn't handle court bookings.
