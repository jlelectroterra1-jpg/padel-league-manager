3. League rules

Each league has its own rules, set by its organiser from a base set provided by the platform. A team accepts those rules (by typing a name) before using its team link. Once a team has accepted, the rules cannot be changed for that league. Matters not covered by the rules are decided by the organiser.

4. Scores and disputes

One team submits a score and the opposing team confirms it. If a score is disputed, the organiser decides it and the organiser's decision is final. The platform simply records what the teams and organiser enter.

5. Fees and payments

  • League activation fee. An organiser pays R50 once per league to activate it. Until it is paid, the league's team links do not open. Once a league is activated, the fee is not refundable.
  • Entry fees. An organiser may set an entry fee per team. The price a team pays is the organiser's fee plus a 20% platform fee, shown as one price on the team link. A team's entry covers both partners.
  • How payments work. Payments are made online through PayFast, by card or instant EFT. The platform does not see or store card details. A team is registered once PayFast confirms its payment.
  • Payouts. We collect entry fees and pay the organiser's share to the organiser after the league's teams have paid.
  • Refunds. Refunds to teams (for example when a team withdraws) are handled by the league organiser.
  • Cut-off date. Each league with an entry fee has a payment cut-off date. After it, a team that has not paid cannot pay on its link, and the organiser decides what happens next.
  • Court fees are paid separately, directly to the court, when booking on the Playtomic app. The platform does not handle court bookings or court fees.
