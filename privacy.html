Padel League Manager

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This explains what personal information Padel League Manager ("we") handles, why, and who can see it. We aim to follow South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

What we collect

  • Organisers: your name, email address, optional phone number and password (stored securely by our sign-in provider, not readable by us), and the leagues you create.
  • Players: the team name and the two players' names that the organiser enters for each team, the team's results and availability, and the name typed when a team accepts the league rules (with the time).
  • Payments: for each payment, the amount, status, date and PayFast's payment reference. We do not see or store card or bank details - those are entered on PayFast's own payment page.
  • Technical: basic information needed to run the site (for example, your browser remembering your team link or login on your own device).

Why we use it

To run leagues: schedule fixtures, record and confirm scores, show league tables, record that rules were accepted, take and track payments, pay organisers their share, and keep the platform secure.

Who can see it

  • The league's organiser sees their league's teams, players, results, rules acceptances and which teams have paid.
  • Teams in the same league see each other's team names, player names, results and (if shared) availability on the team link.
  • The platform owner can see all leagues and payment records in order to run the platform and pay organisers.
  • Service providers that run the platform for us: PayFast (payments) and our database and hosting providers, who process information on our behalf.

We do not sell personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep league records for as long as the league exists and for a reasonable period afterwards. Payment records are kept as long as needed for accounting and tax purposes. An organiser can delete a league, which removes its teams, fixtures and results.

Keeping it safe

The site uses encrypted connections, organiser accounts are protected by passwords, and payments are confirmed by PayFast on our server. No online system is perfectly secure, so keep your password and team link private.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how it is used. Players should first ask their league organiser, who controls the team and player details in their league. You may also complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa.

Children

Organisers are responsible for making sure they have the right to enter the details of any player under 18, including a parent's or guardian's consent where needed.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
